# NMAMIT Examination Guidelines (summary)

This is a summary of the examination rules for NMAM Institute of Technology (NMAMIT), Nitte, compiled from the
B.Tech CSE Regulations & Curriculum 2023 (students admitted from 2023-24). Differences in the 2024-25 B.E. Regulations
(160-credit scheme, students admitted from 2021-22) are noted where relevant. Page numbers are PDF page numbers.
For exam timetables and notifications, students should check with the Controller of Examinations (CoE).

## Structure of assessment

- Every theory course has Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE, 50 marks) and a Semester End Examination (SEE, 50 marks).
- CIE for a regular (non-PBL) course: two mid-semester examinations (40 marks) plus quizzes, tutorials, assignments and seminars (10 marks).
- CIE for PBL / IPCC (integrated professional core) courses: Project Based Learning (50 marks), mid-semester exams (40 marks) and quizzes/assignments (10 marks), weighted 60% theory and 40% PBL/practical.
- Practical-only (0-0-P) courses use a continuous evaluation scheme set by the course instructor and approved by the DUGC, plus an SEE.
- The course instructor must announce the evaluation scheme and the marks-to-grade conversion in the first week of the semester.

(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 11.3-11.5, PDF page 32)

## Eligibility to write the SEE

- At least 85% attendance is required (the Principal can condone up to 10% for valid reasons).
- A student with less than 75% attendance, or CIE marks below 40%, in a course cannot write that course's SEE and gets an 'N' grade.
- Attendance at all CIE and SEE examinations is compulsory. There are no re-examinations.
- A student facing pending disciplinary action cannot write any SEE in that semester.

(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 9.1-9.6 and 11.7, PDF pages 31 and 35)

## Passing standard

- CIE: at least 40% (20 out of 50).
- SEE: at least 40% (20 out of 50).
- Final pass: at least 40% of the course total (CIE + SEE out of 100).

(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 11.6, PDF page 34)

## Exam hall rules and conduct

- Students must be inside the examination hall 20 minutes before the exam starts. This applies to all examinations
  (semester end, supplementary and make-up). No one is allowed in after the exam has started, under any circumstances.
- Any use of unfair means or malpractice in an examination is reported to the Controller of Examinations for action.
- Mobile phone use in the college academic area, plagiarism of any kind, and hacking computer systems are violations
  of the code of conduct. Penalties can include being debarred from an examination.

(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 20, PDF page 45)

## Make-up examination

- A make-up exam is for students who missed the SEE for a valid reason (illness, accident or a family calamity),
  were given an 'I' grade, and had attendance >= 85% and CIE >= 70%. Students with an 'X' grade can also take it.
- Apply to the Controller of Examinations, with supporting documents, within 2 working days of the missed exam.
  Late requests are not accepted.
- Make-up exams are held on dates in the Academic Calendar (during the summer semester) and are of the same standard as the regular SEE.
- Any 'I' or 'X' grade still outstanding after the last scheduled make-up exam becomes an 'F'.
- Under the 2024-25 B.E. Regulations, the make-up exam is allowed only for students with more than 45 CIE marks.

(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 11.8 and 11.12, PDF pages 35-37; 2024-25 B.E. Regulations, section 8.9)

## Backlogs and the summer semester

- A student who met the CIE and attendance requirements but got an F in the SEE can write the SEE again as a backlog
  paper in the following examinations, without re-registering.
- Courses with an 'N' grade must be re-registered (in the summer or a regular semester) to earn the CIE and attendance again.
- The summer / fast-track semester is optional and allows at most 16 credits. Not every course is offered.

(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 11.9, PDF page 36)

## Re-evaluation, grace marks and appeals

- Re-evaluation is allowed only for theory papers. Apply within the stipulated time and pay the fee. The marks after re-evaluation are final.
- Grace marks: up to 1% of the maximum total marks or 10 marks, whichever is less (at most 5 marks per course), given only if they make the student pass.
- Grade appeal: course instructor first, then the DUGC, then the Department Academic Appeals Board (DAAB) with a fee
  (refunded if the appeal is upheld).

(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 11.11, 11.13 and 16, PDF pages 37-39)

## Results, medals and ranks

- Results are declared within 2 weeks of the last examination (2024-25 B.E. Regulations, section 1.7).
- SGPA and CGPA are calculated to two decimal places. Each student gets a grade card every semester.
- Merit certificates and university medals are only for students who finished in the minimum time (4 years, or 3 for
  lateral entry) and passed every semester at the first attempt. Students with W, N, I, X or F grades who passed later
  in supplementary or make-up exams are not eligible for a gold medal or merit certificate.

(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 11.10, 13 and 19, PDF pages 37-38 and 44)
