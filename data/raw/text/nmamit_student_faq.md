# NMAMIT Student FAQ

Frequently asked academic questions for students of NMAM Institute of Technology (NMAMIT), Nitte,
Karkala Taluk, Udupi District, Karnataka. Every answer was compiled from the official NMAMIT
regulation documents. Page numbers are PDF page numbers.

- "2023 B.Tech Regulations" = B.Tech CSE Regulations & Curriculum 2023 (students admitted from 2023-24, NITTE Deemed to be University).
- "2024-25 B.E. Regulations" = NMAMIT Regulations 2024-25, 160-credit scheme (students admitted from 2021-22).

If a rule differs between the two, both versions are given. For the latest official position, students
should always confirm with their Faculty Advisor, the department, or the Controller of Examinations.

## About NMAMIT

### What is NMAMIT?
NMAM Institute of Technology (NMAMIT) was established in 1986 at Nitte. It is an off-campus centre of
NITTE (Deemed to be University), accredited by NAAC with an 'A+' grade, and recognised by AICTE, New Delhi.
(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, Preamble, PDF page 9)

### Which B.Tech programmes does NMAMIT offer?
NMAMIT offers fourteen UG programmes: Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AM), Artificial
Intelligence & Data Science (AD), Biotechnology (BT), Computer & Communication Engineering (CC),
Computer Science & Engineering (CS), Civil Engineering (CV), Electronics & Communication Engineering (EC),
Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EE), Information Science & Engineering (IS), Mechanical Engineering (ME),
Robotics & Artificial Intelligence (RI), Computer Science & Engineering - Cyber Security (CB),
Electronics Engineering - VLSI Design and Technology (VT) and Electronics & Communication - Advanced
Communication Technology (AC). (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, Preamble, PDF page 9)

## Credits and registration

### How many credits are needed to get the B.Tech degree?
A regular 4-year student must earn 160 credits. A lateral entry student must earn 120 credits. A CGPA of at
least 5.00 at the end of the programme is also required. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 17.1,
PDF page 40; the 2024-25 B.E. Regulations, section 14.1, have the same 160/120 credits and CGPA >= 5.00 rules)

### How many credits does a lateral entry student need?
A lateral entry student (a diploma holder admitted directly to the 3rd semester / second year) must earn 120 credits
for the B.Tech degree, within a maximum of 6 years. Lateral entry students also need 75 AICTE activity points instead
of 100, and must complete the mandatory non-credit mathematics bridge courses.
(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 14.5, 14.6 and 17.1, PDF pages 38-40)

### How many credits must I have before submitting the final-year (8th semester) major project?
A regular student must have earned at least 122 credits to submit the B.Tech Major Project report in the 8th semester.
A lateral entry student needs at least 88 credits. Students are normally expected to complete at least 85% of their
credits by the end of the 7th semester. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 8.5 and 14.4-14.5, PDF pages 30 and 38)

### How many credits can I register for in a semester?
Under the 2023 B.Tech Regulations, a student can register for between 16 and 28 credits per semester.
A student on academic probation (CGPA below 5.00) can register for at most 16 credits per semester until the CGPA goes back above 5.0.
(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 8.2 and 14.2)

### What is the maximum time allowed to complete the degree?
8 years (16 semesters) from admission for regular students, and 6 years (12 semesters) for lateral entry students.
(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 17.3.3, PDF page 44)

### What is a B.Tech (Honours) degree?
A student who earns at least 18 additional credits in their own major discipline earns the 'Honours' credential.
A B.Tech with Minor needs 18 additional credits (178 in total) in another discipline.
(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 3.1 and 17.2.1)

## Attendance

### What is the minimum attendance requirement at NMAMIT?
Students must have at least 85% attendance in each subject. The Principal can condone up to 10% for reasons
such as medical grounds, university-level sports, cultural activities, seminars, workshops and paper
presentations. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 9.1, PDF page 31; 2024-25 B.E. Regulations, section 6.1)

### What happens if my attendance is below 75%?
A student with less than 75% attendance in a course cannot write the Semester End Examination (SEE) for that
course and gets an 'N' grade. The student must re-register for the course and repeat it the next time it is
offered, in a main (odd/even) semester or a summer/supplementary semester.
(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 9.4-9.5; 2024-25 B.E. Regulations, section 6.4)

### How will I know my attendance status?
The college tells students their attendance position in the first week of every month, so they can make up any
shortage. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 9.3)

## Examinations and evaluation

### How are marks divided between CIE and SEE?
Each course is evaluated 50% through Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE, 50 marks) and 50% through the
Semester End Examination (SEE, 50 marks).
For non-PBL courses, CIE = quizzes/tutorials/assignments/seminars (10 marks) + mid-semester examinations (40 marks).
For PBL/IPCC (integrated) courses, CIE includes Project Based Learning (50 marks), mid-semester examinations
(40 marks) and quizzes/assignments (10 marks), weighted 60% theory and 40% PBL/practical.
(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 11.3, PDF page 32)

### What is the passing criterion?
A student needs at least 40% in CIE (20 out of 50), at least 40% in SEE (20 out of 50), and at least 40% of the
total (CIE + SEE) to pass a course. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 11.6, PDF page 34;
2024-25 B.E. Regulations, section 8.6)

### Is there a re-examination if I fail?
No. There is no re-examination in the credit system. Students with an F grade who have met the CIE and
attendance requirements may write the SEE again as a backlog paper in later examinations. Students with an
'N' grade must re-register for the course. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 11.5(vi) and 11.9)

### What is the grading system?
Absolute grading on a 10-point scale:
- 90% and above: O (Outstanding), 10 points
- 80-89%: A+ (Excellent), 9 points
- 70-79%: A (Very Good), 8 points
- 60-69%: B+ (Good), 7 points
- 55-59%: B (Above Average), 6 points
- 50-54%: C (Average), 5 points
- 40-49%: P (Pass), 4 points
- 0-39%: F (Fail), 0 points
- Absent: AB, 0 points

(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 11.7, PDF page 35)

### How are SGPA and CGPA calculated?
SGPA = sum of (course credits x grade points) / sum of course credits, over all courses in that semester.
CGPA = the same formula over all courses up to that semester, leaving out courses with F grades.
Both are calculated to two decimal places. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 12-13, PDF page 38)

### How is CGPA converted to a class (Distinction / First Class)?
Under the 2023 B.Tech Regulations: CGPA 7.00 and above = First Class with Distinction; 6.00-6.99 = First Class;
5.00-5.99 = Second Class. Percentage equivalent = CGPA x 10. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 15, PDF page 39)
Under the 2024-25 B.E. Regulations: Grade point >= 7.75 = Distinction; >= 6.75 = First Class; < 6.75 = Second Class.
Percentage = (GPA - 0.75) x 10. (Source: 2024-25 B.E. Regulations, section 12, PDF page 19)

### What is a make-up examination and who can write it?
A make-up exam is for students who missed the SEE for valid reasons (illness, accident or a family calamity)
and were given an 'I' grade, and for students with an 'X' grade. The student must apply to the Controller of
Examinations, with documents, within 2 working days of the missed exam. Make-up exams are held on dates in
the Academic Calendar (during the summer semester under the 2023 regulations). Any 'I' or 'X' grade still
outstanding after the last make-up exam becomes an 'F'. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 11.8 and 11.12, PDF pages 36-37)

### What do the grades N, I, W, X, PP and NP mean?
- N: not allowed to write the SEE because attendance was below 75% or CIE was below 40%. Grade point 0.
- I: absent from the SEE for a valid reason, with attendance >= 85% and CIE >= 70%.
- W: withdrew from the course before the prescribed date, on faculty advice.
- X: attendance >= 85% and CIE >= 70%, but SEE performance would have resulted in an F.
- PP / NP: Passed / Not Passed in mandatory non-credit courses. PP is required in every mandatory course to get the degree.

(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 11.7-11.10)

### Can I apply for re-evaluation?
Yes, for theory papers only. Apply within the stipulated time and pay the prescribed fee. The marks after
re-evaluation are final. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 11.11, PDF page 37)

### Are grace marks given?
Grace marks of up to 1% of the maximum total marks of the registered courses, or 10 marks, whichever is less,
can be awarded to failed course(s), with at most 5 marks per course, provided this makes the student pass
those course(s). (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 11.13, PDF page 37)

### How do I appeal against a grade?
First talk to the course instructor, then the Department Undergraduate Committee (DUGC). If the issue is still
not resolved, appeal to the Department Academic Appeals Board (DAAB) before the date in the Academic Calendar
and pay the prescribed fee. The fee is refunded if the appeal is upheld.
(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 16, PDF page 39)

### What is the summer / fast-track semester?
An optional semester where students can re-register for courses (for example, courses with an 'N' grade, or to
improve CIE in courses with W/I/X grades). A student can register for at most 16 credits in a summer semester.
Not every course is offered. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 11.9, PDF page 36)

## Promotion, probation and termination

### Will I be promoted to the next year if I have backlogs?
Under the 2023 B.Tech Regulations, every student is promoted to the next semester or year whatever their
academic performance. If the CGPA falls below 5.00, the student is put on Academic Probation (at most 16 credits
per semester). To submit the 8th-semester major project, a regular student needs at least 122 credits
(a lateral entry student needs at least 88). (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 14.1-14.5, PDF page 38)
Under the 2024-25 B.E. Regulations, a student can move from first year to second year with no more than four
F grades in the two semesters of first year. (Source: 2024-25 B.E. Regulations, section 11.3)

### When can a student be terminated from the programme?
Under the 2023 B.Tech Regulations, a student must leave the programme if they: (i) do not reach a CGPA of 5.0
by the end of 8 years (6 for lateral entry); (ii) do not earn 160 credits (120 for lateral entry) within that
time; (iii) are absent for more than six weeks at a time in a semester without approved leave; or (iv) fail to
meet the college's standards of discipline. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 14.6, PDF page 39)

### Can I take a break (temporary withdrawal) from the programme?
A student may withdraw temporarily once during the course, for one semester, because of prolonged illness or a
grave calamity in the family. The student must apply within 6 weeks of the start of the semester, with supporting
documents and the endorsement of a parent or guardian, and must have no dues.
(Source: 2024-25 B.E. Regulations, section 7.1; 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 10.1)

## Activity points

### What are AICTE activity points and how many do I need?
Every regular student must earn 100 activity points through the AICTE Activity Point Programme to get the degree.
Lateral entry students must earn 75. The activities can be done at any time during the programme (weekends,
holidays). Activity points are non-credit: they do not affect SGPA/CGPA or promotion. If the required points are
not earned before the 8th-semester exams begin, the 8th-semester grade card, and therefore the degree, is held
back until they are earned. (Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, section 17.1(d), PDF page 40;
2024-25 B.E. Regulations, section 14.1(e))

## Internships and projects

### Is an internship mandatory at NMAMIT?
Yes. B.Tech students (2023 regulations) must complete Internship-I (2 weeks, 80-90 hours, intra-institutional,
during first year) and Internship-II (8 weeks, during the vacations between the IV and VII semesters), a total of
10 weeks and 10 credits. A student who does not complete the internship is declared failed in that course.
See the "NMAMIT Internship Guidelines" document for details.
(Source: 2023 B.Tech Regulations, sections 11.5 and 17.3.3, PDF pages 32-33 and 43-44)

## Scholarships

### What scholarships are available for NMAMIT students?
The college calendar lists scholarships for SC/ST students, for Category I, 2A, 3A and 3B students, for GSB and
Brahmin (EWS) students, for minority students, and the Beedi scholarship. Students apply online through the SSP
(State Scholarship Portal) or NSP (scholarships.gov.in). Scholarship details are put up on the notice board near
the College Academic Section. Students who apply online must also submit a hard copy with attested supporting
documents to the academic section on time. (Source: CSE VII & VIII Semester College Calendar 2024-25, PDF page 41)
